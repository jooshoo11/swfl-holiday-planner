## 🎃 Fall & Halloween Festivities (October 2026)

### 1. Prana Farms Annual Pumpkin Festival
- **Where:** 6026 Sweden Blvd, Punta Gorda, FL 33982 *(~7 miles • 12 mins)*
- **When:** Oct 15 – Oct 25, 2026 (Thursdays – Sundays, 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
- **Admission:** ~$12 (Kids under 2 Free)
- **Highlights:** Giant pumpkin patch, butterfly encounter tent, farm petting zoo, bounce houses, barrel train rides, and autumn photo stations.
- **Family Tip:** Wear closed-toe shoes; morning hours around 10:00 AM have cooler weather and the best photo lighting.

### 2. City of Punta Gorda Fall Festival
- **Where:** Laishley Park, 100 Nesbit St, Punta Gorda, FL 33950 *(~5 miles • 8 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, October 17, 2026 (12:00 PM – 4:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Waterfront park trick-or-treating stations, inflatables, lawn games, costume contest with age-group prizes, and food trucks.
- **Family Tip:** Stroller-friendly paved harbor walk. Bring a candy tote and sunscreen.

### 3. Halloween Notte: Family Fun & Haunts
- **Where:** Charlotte County Fairgrounds, 2333 El Jobean Rd, Port Charlotte, FL 33948 *(~9 miles • 14 mins)*
- **When:** Oct 16–18, 23–25, & 29–31, 2026 (6:00 PM – 10:00 PM)
- **Admission:** $15 (Kids 3 & under Free)
- **Highlights:** Day/twilight family midway with pumpkin patch, petting zoo, inflatables, and safe trick-or-treating; haunted walk-through zones for older teens/adults.
- **Family Tip:** Younger kids and toddlers should stick to the illuminated family midway area right at 6:00 PM opening.

### 4. Venice Nights Market & Music (Halloween Edition)
- **Where:** Maxine Barritt Park, 1800 Harbor Dr S, Venice, FL 34285 *(~27 miles • 32 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, October 17, 2026 (5:00 PM – 10:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Coastal evening on the Gulf lawn featuring a costumed pet parade, youth costume parade, live music, and food trucks.
- **Family Tip:** Bring lawn chairs or blankets to catch the Gulf sunset while listening to the band with Venice family.

### 5. North Port Trick-or-Treat & Pumpkin Trail
- **Where:** George Mullen Activity Center & City Center Green, 4956 City Center Blvd, North Port, FL 34286 *(~16 miles • 20 mins)*
- **When:** Friday, October 23, 2026 (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** The signature North Port family tradition! Illuminated Pumpkin Trail with dozens of booths giving candy, DJ Monster Mash Pit, and costume contests.
- **Family Tip:** Very popular! Carpool with North Port family or arrive 20 minutes before 5:00 PM to secure parking.

### 6. Babcock Ranch Halloween Nights
- **Where:** Founder's Square, 42850 Crescent Loop, Babcock Ranch, FL 33982 *(~18 miles • 22 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, October 24, 2026 (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Lighted decorated golf cart parade, lakeside trick-or-treat stops, family fun zone, and music around the boardwalk.
- **Family Tip:** Toddlers love the golf cart parade! Secure a viewing spot along Crescent Loop by 5:45 PM.

### 7. Wellen Park Spooktacular
- **Where:** Downtown Wellen, 19745 Wellen Pkwy, Venice, FL 34293 *(~21 miles • 24 mins)*
- **When:** Friday, October 30, 2026 (4:00 PM – 7:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Wide pedestrian promenade trick-or-treating at lakefront merchants, live DJ, games, and costume contest.
- **Family Tip:** Ideal midpoint meet-up between Punta Gorda and Venice! 100% stroller- and wagon-friendly paved walkways.

### 8. Historic Downtown Venice Halloween Strut
- **Where:** West Venice Ave & Miami Ave, Venice, FL 34285 *(~26 miles • 30 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, October 31, 2026 (Halloween, 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Daytime downtown stroll where costumed kids trick-or-treat at local merchants under the palm trees.
- **Family Tip:** Wraps up at 6:00 PM, giving you time for early family dinner before traditional neighborhood trick-or-treating.

## 🎄 Christmas & Winter Holiday Celebrations (Nov – Dec 2026)

### 9. Grand Lighting of Fishermen's Village
- **Where:** Fishermen's Village, 1200 W Retta St, Punta Gorda, FL 33950 *(~6 miles • 9 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, November 14, 2026 (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM; Lighting at dusk)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Southwest Florida's premier season kickoff! Watch over 1 million lights turn on simultaneously, welcome Santa and Mrs. Claus, and enjoy carolers and bagpipers.
- **Family Tip:** Arrive by 4:30 PM to park in the main lot before it fills.

### 10. Festival of Lights at Fishermen's Village
- **Where:** Fishermen's Village, 1200 W Retta St, Punta Gorda, FL 33950 *(~6 miles • 9 mins)*
- **When:** Daily, November 15 – December 31, 2026 (Lights on from 5:30 PM nightly)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Over 1 million illuminated lights, 30+ themed trees in the concourses, Santa photo visits, waterfront dining, and live holiday music.
- **Family Tip:** Visit on Monday through Wednesday evenings for relaxed stroller walks and effortless parking.

### 11. 50th Annual Venice Holiday Parade ("Golden Traditions")
- **Where:** Historic Downtown Venice (West Venice Ave to Venice High), Venice, FL 34285 *(~26 miles • 30 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, November 28, 2026 (Thanksgiving Weekend • Pre-parade 3:00 PM, Parade 6:30 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Landmark 50th Golden Anniversary evening parade with 100+ lighted floats, marching bands, dancers, and Santa.
- **Family Tip:** Families set out folding chairs along Venice Ave early in the afternoon. Great holiday weekend event with Venice family!

### 12. King Fisher Christmas Light Canal Cruises
- **Where:** Fishermen's Village Marina, Punta Gorda, FL 33950 *(~6 miles • 9 mins)*
- **When:** Daily throughout December 2026 (Evenings at 6:00, 7:30, and 9:00 PM)
- **Admission:** ~$28–$32 Adults / $18–$22 Kids
- **Highlights:** 60-minute narrated boat cruise through Punta Gorda Isles canals to view multi-million dollar homes and docks competing in neighborhood holiday light displays.
- **Family Tip:** Book weeks in advance at [kingfisherfleet.com](https://www.kingfisherfleet.com) as weekend cruises always sell out early. Bring light sweaters for the water breeze.

### 13. Wellen Wonderland Tree Lighting Celebration
- **Where:** Downtown Wellen, 19745 Wellen Pkwy, Venice, FL 34293 *(~21 miles • 24 mins)*
- **When:** Friday, December 4, 2026 (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Lighting of the 32-foot lakeside Christmas tree, Santa and Mrs. Claus photos, "Cottages for Kids" gingerbread village, and carolers.
- **Family Tip:** Huge lawn areas for energetic toddlers to play. Bring picnic blankets.

### 14. North Port Poinsettia Parade & Tree Lighting Festival
- **Where:** City Center Front Green, 4970 City Center Blvd, North Port, FL 34286 *(~16 miles • 20 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, December 5, 2026 (Festival 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM • Parade at 5:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** North Port's flagship holiday celebration! Lighted floats down Sumter Blvd ("Sleigh the Night Away"), official tree lighting at City Hall, Santa's workshop, and craft vendors.
- **Family Tip:** Sumter Blvd closes around 4:30 PM; use Price Blvd to reach the parking lots.

### 15. Venice Christmas Boat Parade
- **Where:** Venice Intracoastal Waterway (Albee Rd Bridge south to Circus Bridge), Venice, FL *(~27 miles • 32 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, December 5, 2026 (Starts at 6:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE Public Viewing**
- **Highlights:** Dazzling aquatic procession of decorated powerboats, sailboats, and yachts illuminated from stem to stern.
- **Family Tip:** Stroller-friendly public shoreline viewing along the Venetian Waterway Park trail or near the North Jetty.

### 16. Wellen Park Holiday Glow Bike Ride & Dueling Pianos
- **Where:** Downtown Wellen & Solis Hall Stage, Venice, FL *(~21 miles • 24 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, December 5, 2026 (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Family slow roll bicycle/stroller parade around the lake wrapped in glow sticks and string lights, followed by festive dueling pianos.
- **Family Tip:** Clip battery-powered fairy lights to your kids' strollers or trikes!

### 17. The Mayor's Hometown Christmas & Christmas Walk
- **Where:** Centennial Park & Downtown Venice, FL 34285 *(~26 miles • 30 mins)*
- **When:** Thursday, December 10, 2026 (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Classic storybook holiday evening! Meet Santa in the park gazebo, enjoy live youth choir performances, sip warm cider, and view festive decorated storefronts.
- **Family Tip:** Centennial Park has a playground right next to the gazebo to keep little ones engaged.

### 18. Peace River Gardens Aglow
- **Where:** Peace River Botanical & Sculpture Gardens, 5827 Riverside Dr, Punta Gorda, FL 33982 *(~8 miles • 12 mins)*
- **When:** Dec 11–13 & Dec 18–20, 2026 (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM)
- **Admission:** ~$15 Adults / $7 Kids (3 and under Free)
- **Highlights:** Thousands of glowing fairy lights and botanical light sculptures along boardwalk paths on the Peace River, plus s'mores roasting and hot chocolate.
- **Family Tip:** Smooth boardwalks are very stroller-friendly. Pre-book timed tickets online at [peacerivergardens.org](https://peacerivergardens.org).

### 19. Annual Punta Gorda Christmas Boat Parade
- **Where:** Punta Gorda Isles Waterfront Canals / Rim Canal *(~6 miles • 9 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, December 19, 2026 (Starts at 6:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE Public Viewing**
- **Highlights:** The beloved "Saturday Night Before Christmas Eve" boat parade! 4 miles through protected canals with lavishly decorated yachts and music.
- **Family Tip:** Public shoreline viewing with parking at Ponce de Leon Park or along the rim canal bridges.

### 20. Christmas Carols by the Gulf
- **Where:** Maxine Barritt Park Pavilion, 1800 Harbor Dr S, Venice, FL 34285 *(~27 miles • 32 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, December 19, 2026 (5:00 PM – 7:00 PM)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Community sunset sing-along on the Gulf lawn. Song sheets, holiday cookies, and hot cider provided.
- **Family Tip:** Bring lawn chairs, cozy sweaters, and glow sticks for the kids as darkness falls over the beach.

### 21. Fishermen's Village New Year's Eve & Fireworks
- **Where:** Fishermen's Village, 1200 W Retta St, Punta Gorda, FL 33950 *(~6 miles • 9 mins)*
- **When:** Thursday, December 31, 2026 (6:00 PM – 12:30 AM; Fireworks at Midnight)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Southwest Florida's top family NYE festival. Multiple stages of music, face painting, balloon twisters, magicians, and midnight harbor fireworks.
- **Family Tip:** If celebrating with younger toddlers, the 6:00 PM – 8:30 PM window is filled with games and light displays without the late midnight exhaustion!
