## 🎃 Fall & Halloween Festivities (October 2026)

### 1. Hunsader Farms 35th Annual Pumpkin Festival
- **Where:** 5500 C.R. 675, Bradenton, FL 34211 *(East Manatee County • ~48 mi)*
- **When:** Oct 10–11, 17–18, & 24–25, 2026 (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
- **Admission:** $15 (Kids 10 & under Free) • $5 Parking
- **Highlights:** The Suncoast's premier agricultural fall festival! Massive pumpkin patch, craft booths, corn maze, live bluegrass and country bands, monster truck rides, circus stunt shows, pony rides, and homemade pumpkin pies.
- **Family Tip:** Bring cash for parking/food. Strollers and wagons navigate the farm easily. Arrive before 10:30 AM to beat traffic on CR 675.

### 2. Prana Farms Annual Pumpkin Festival
- **Where:** 6026 Sweden Blvd, Punta Gorda, FL 33982 *(~7 miles • 12 mins)*
- **When:** Oct 15 – Oct 25, 2026 (Thursdays – Sundays, 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
- **Admission:** ~$12 (Kids under 2 Free)
- **Highlights:** Giant pumpkin patch, butterfly encounter tent, farm petting zoo, bounce houses, barrel train rides, and autumn photo stations.
- **Family Tip:** Wear closed-toe shoes; morning hours around 10:00 AM have cooler weather and the best photo lighting.

## 🎄 Christmas & Winter Holiday Celebrations (Nov – Dec 2026)

### 11. Grand Lighting of Fishermen's Village
- **Where:** Fishermen's Village, 1200 W Retta St, Punta Gorda, FL 33950 *(~6 miles • 9 mins)*
- **When:** Saturday, November 14, 2026 (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM; Lighting at dusk)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Southwest Florida's premier season kickoff! Watch over 1 million lights turn on simultaneously, welcome Santa and Mrs. Claus, and enjoy carolers and bagpipers.
- **Family Tip:** Arrive by 4:30 PM to park in the main lot.

### 12. Festival of Lights at Fishermen's Village
- **Where:** Fishermen's Village, 1200 W Retta St, Punta Gorda, FL 33950 *(~6 miles • 9 mins)*
- **When:** Daily, November 15 – December 31, 2026 (Lights on from 5:30 PM nightly)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Highlights:** Over 1 million illuminated lights, 30+ themed trees in the concourses, Santa photo visits, waterfront dining, and live holiday music.
- **Family Tip:** Visit on Monday through Wednesday evenings for relaxed stroller walks and effortless parking.

### 13. Holidays at UTC & The Rink (Festival of Lights)

- **Highlights:** Over 4 million choreographed lights set to seasonal music, outdoor real-ice skating rink (The Rink at UTC), free outdoor holiday movies under the stars, Saturday fireworks, and horse-drawn carriage rides.

### 14. 50th Annual Venice Holiday Parade ("Golden Traditions")
