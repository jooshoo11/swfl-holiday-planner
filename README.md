## 🌟 How to Use

### 1. Open Instantly in Any Web Browser
Simply double-click **[`index.html`](file:///C:/Users/user/swfl_holiday_planner/index.html)** or right-click and choose **Open With -> Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge / Firefox**.
- No server or command-line installation required.
- Works 100% offline.

### 2. Install as a Progressive Web App (PWA)
You can install this app directly onto your phone, tablet, or computer like a native app:
- **Online URL**: [https://jooshoo11.github.io/swfl-holiday-planner/](https://jooshoo11.github.io/swfl-holiday-planner/)
- **On Android & Chrome / Edge**: Click the **"📲 Install App"** button in the header bar (or browser menu -> *Install App* / *Add to Home screen*).
- **On iPhone / iPad (Safari)**: Tap the **Share** button (box with upward arrow) -> scroll down and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
- **Offline Capable**: The app runs offline using a built-in Service Worker cache, so you can check events even when on the go with poor reception.

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **🧭 Proximity Radar (Centered on Punta Gorda 33983)**:
   - **Closest Tier (0–9 miles / 8–15 mins)**: Punta Gorda & Port Charlotte (Fishermen's Village, Laishley Park, Prana Farms, Charlotte County Fairgrounds).
   - **Family Tier (~14–17 miles / 18–22 mins)**: North Port (City Center Green, George Mullen Center).
   - **Midway Point (~18–21 miles / 22–25 mins)**: Wellen Park (Downtown Wellen lakefront promenade).
   - **Venice Family Tier (~25–28 miles / 30–35 mins)**: Historic Downtown Venice & Maxine Barritt Park.

2. **🎃 Full Fall & Winter Season Coverage**:
   - **October (Fall & Halloween)**: Prana Farms Pumpkin Fest, Punta Gorda Fall Fest at Laishley Park, Halloween Notte, North Port Pumpkin Trail, Wellen Park Spooktacular, Downtown Venice Halloween Strut.
   - **November & December (Christmas & Holiday Lights)**: Grand Lighting of Fishermen's Village (1M+ lights), King Fisher Canal Cruises, Venice 50th Annual Holiday Parade, Wellen Wonderland Tree Lighting, North Port Poinsettia Parade, Venice Christmas Boat Parade, Peace River Gardens Aglow, Punta Gorda Christmas Boat Parade.
   - **New Year's Eve**: Fishermen's Village Harbor Fireworks & Family Celebration.

3. **📅 Dual View (Cards & Interactive Calendar)**:
   - **Cards Grid**: View detailed descriptions, dates, hours, costs, stroller advice, parking insights, Google Maps directions, and one-click `.ics` calendar download.
   - **Interactive Calendar**: Browse October, November, December 2026, and January 2027 by date to see day-by-day festivities.

4. **❤️ Personal Holiday Itinerary & Calendar Export**:
   - Click the heart icon on any event to bookmark it into your personal family holiday schedule.
   - Export your entire saved itinerary with one click into a standard `.ics` file that imports directly into **Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook**.
   - Hit **Print Itinerary** for a printer-friendly layout for your fridge or planner.

5. **➕ Custom Family Events**:
   - Add private family get-togethers (e.g., Thanksgiving Dinner in Venice, school concerts, neighbor light walks) directly to the calendar and card feed. Everything persists automatically in your browser's local storage.

6. **🎨 Themes**:
   - Switch between **Autumn Glow**, **Winter Wonderland**, and **Coastal Festive**.
